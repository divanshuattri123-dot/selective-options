Finite stop occurred before full acknowledgement and no new timeout marker was recorded. 25,560 unique rows/outbox entries were retained.

The last completed response took 10.987262 seconds, including 9.1504206 seconds normalizing. These are disjoint client monotonic intervals, not CPU cycles or pure wire latency. They exclude limiter/send/progress/close time, and no durable timing is available for the interrupted request at stop. The view remains `OPTION_STREAM_INACTIVE`.
